# EXT-06: Talking-head reaction overlays ("Sick kill!" from your friends' avatars)
Owner request: reuse our team's talking-head avatars and cloned voices (made in our Village project) as **reaction clips** overlaid on montage moments.
Build `full_app/reactions/`:
- A **reaction pack** format: a folder with `pack.json` listing clips `{id, speaker, label, text, file, duration, background}`. Labels: sick_kill, clutch, bruh, fail, funny, wholesome, hype, roast. Packs live in `full_app/reaction_packs/<name>/`, which must be **gitignored** (real people's faces and voices are never committed). Ship only a tiny synthetic demo pack generated by a script (colored circle with a moving mouth shape + a beep tone) for tests.
- **Selector:** given a clip's label/score/mode (nice vs mean) choose a reaction (variety, no repeats in a row, speaker rotation), preferring the VLM's suggested reaction label when present.
- **Overlay renderer:** picture-in-picture talking head in a corner with a circular/rounded mask (key out a plain light background), drop shadow and pop-in/out animation, timed to the clip's peak moment; duck game audio under the voice line; optional speaker-name lower third. Works for landscape and vertical presets (corner positions per preset).
- `reactions.render_overlay(base_clip, reaction, preset) -> path` plus an integration hook the backend can call.
Tests with the synthetic pack: overlay timing, audio presence/ducking, positions per preset (ffprobe checks). Document the pack format and how a pack is built from Village talking heads in docs/reactions.md (the integrator generates the real pack separately).
